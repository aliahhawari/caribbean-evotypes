Get Involved

Understanding prostate cancer in African-Caribbean men to improve care for future generations.

Our research in Jamaica and the UK explores how prostate cancer develops in African-Caribbean men, to improve outcomes for our community.

Learn About Our Research Get Involved

Why this study matters

👥

Higher risk

African-Caribbean men are more likely to develop aggressive prostate cancer.

?

Not enough answers

Too little research has focused on how prostate cancer develops in our community.

🧬

Our approach

We study tumour genomics alongside lifestyle and family history to understand why.

♡

Better outcomes

Our goal is to build knowledge that can support better detection, treatment and care.
