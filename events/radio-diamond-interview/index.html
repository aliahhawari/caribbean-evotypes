COMMUNITY OUTREACH

Taking the conversation
to the airwaves

6 October 2026 · Radio Diamond

Ahead of our community event on 11 October, members of our research and community team joined Radio Diamond to talk about prostate cancer, our research and why conversations with African-Caribbean communities matter.

WHY WE WENT ON AIR

Reaching our community

Five days before our Let's Talk About Prostate Cancer: A Community Conversation event, we joined Radio Diamond to introduce our study and invite people from the community to come along, meet the team and join the conversation.

During the interview, we talked about the science behind our research, why we are studying prostate cancer in African-Caribbean men, prostate cancer in Jamaica, and health beliefs and experiences within our communities, including discussion around self-medication.

Radio gave us a way to speak directly with people beyond the university and hospital. The response was encouraging, with a strong community turnout at our event on 11 October.

BEHIND THE MIC

Taking research into the conversation

Radio Diamond gave us the opportunity to talk about our research in a different way: directly, openly and as part of a wider conversation with the community.

It was also a chance to hear different perspectives on prostate cancer, from the science behind the study to experiences and health beliefs within African-Caribbean communities.

FROM THE RADIO TO THE COMMUNITY

The conversation continued five days later

On 11 October 2026, we held Let's Talk About Prostate Cancer: A Community Conversation in Stockport. The event brought researchers, clinicians, patient advocates and members of the African-Caribbean community together to talk openly about prostate cancer and research.

The turnout reinforced something important for us: meaningful engagement means taking research beyond universities and hospitals and meeting communities in the places and spaces they already trust.
