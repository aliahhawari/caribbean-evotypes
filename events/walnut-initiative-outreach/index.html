About the event

This community event brings together Black men, their families and friends, community members and the research team behind our prostate cancer study.

We will share more about our research, talk about prostate cancer and hear from members of the community about their experiences, questions and priorities.

It is an opportunity to meet the research team, from UK and Jamaica, learn more about the study and be part of an open conversation about how research can better serve Black communities.

Light refreshments will be provided.

What to expect
