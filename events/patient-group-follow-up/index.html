About the event

Following our first patient discussion group, we invited contributors back for a follow-up session to continue the conversation and show how their earlier feedback is helping to shape our work.

We will share updates on the study and ask contributors to review some of the materials we are developing to communicate our research, including our website and study brochure. We wanted to understand whether the information was clear, accessible and relevant, and what we could do to make it more useful for patients and the wider community.

The session also gave us an opportunity to continue listening to contributors, hear their views on how they would like to stay involved, and discuss how we can keep the community informed as the research progresses.

Light refreshments will be provided.
