WHAT DOES THIS MEAN?

What is public involvement in research?

Public involvement means doing research with people and communities, rather than research being done only to or for them.

People can help by sharing their experiences, reviewing our information and materials, helping us communicate clearly, and advising us on how best to work with communities.

?

Why do you sometimes see the term PPIE?

Researchers sometimes call this Patient and Public Involvement and Engagement (PPIE).

It simply means involving patients, families and members of the public in research and working together to make research better.

You do not need any research experience to get involved.
